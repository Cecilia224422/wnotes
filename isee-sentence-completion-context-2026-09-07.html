3 Why

Certify means to officially confirm that something is true. Elaborate means to add information or details.certify 是正式確認某件事為真；elaborate 是增加資訊或細節。

Why can’t certify do the job in the original sentence?為什麼原句不能用 certify？

1 Notice

2 Do

Which word partnership means “add extra earnings”?哪一個搭配表示「增加額外收入」？

3 Why

Retain means to keep what is already there. Supplement means to add something extra to improve or complete it.retain 是保留已經有的東西；supplement 是再加上一些，讓原本的東西更足夠或完整。
