🔎 You first

Your chain, exactly as you wrote it:你的鏈條，一字不改：

Step 1

A question that asks how or why is a chain question. It is not asking what happened — it is asking how one thing led to the next.問 how 或 why 的題目就是「因果鏈題」。它問的不是發生了什麼，而是一件事怎麼帶出下一件。

Telling a friend how you got a bruise: "I fell" is not the story. Where were you? What did you trip on? A chain question wants the whole run.跟朋友講你怎麼撞出瘀青：「我跌倒了」不算故事。你在哪裡？被什麼絆到？因果鏈題要的是整段過程。

👉 Tap the part of the question that makes this a chain question.點出讓這題變成因果鏈題的那個部分。

Step 2

You did put a link in the middle: changed the climate. True — but it is the same kind of word as "very bad" on page 121: the reader cannot see it. The lesson swaps it for two things you can see — dust and sunlight: the crash threw dust up, the dust blocked the sun.你中間確實有寫一環：changed the climate。是對的——但它跟 p.121 的「very bad」是同一種字：讀的人看不到畫面。課文把它換成兩個看得見的東西——塵埃和陽光：撞擊揚起塵埃，塵埃擋住太陽。

"The climate changed" is the size of a whole news headline. Zoom in: a huge dust cloud parked over the sky for years — the world's longest cloudy day.「氣候變了」大得像一則新聞標題。拉近看：一整片巨大的塵埃雲停在天空上好幾年——史上最長的一個陰天。

So — when the sunlight is blocked, what stops working first?所以——陽光被擋住的時候，最先停擺的是什麼？

Step 3

One more thing to fix. Your sentence ends in the past — died — but the middle slipped into now: "there is no food".還有一個地方要修。你的句子結尾是過去式 died，但中間滑回了現在式：there is no food。

Dinosaurs are not out there being hungry right now. The whole story is over, so every verb has to look back.恐龍現在並沒有在外面餓肚子。整個故事早就結束了，所以每個動詞都要回頭看。

🔎 You first

"Very bad" is a judgement, not an observation. Science answers report what you could see happen.「很糟」是一個評價，不是觀察。科學的答案要寫「看得到發生了什麼」。
