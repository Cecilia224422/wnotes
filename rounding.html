🔑 The one idea behind all five blanks五個空格背後的同一個想法

Estimate = round first, then calculate. You already rounded 27 numbers perfectly on page 1 — now use that same skill on the word problems.估算 ＝ 先四捨五入，再計算。你在第一頁已經完美地四捨五入了 27 個數——現在把同一招用在應用題上。

  1. Read the last sentence first — what is the question asking for?先讀最後一句——題目到底在問什麼？
  2. Underline the key word: difference · increased · half · minimum · for every 1,000.把關鍵字畫底線：difference、increased、half、minimum、for every 1,000。
  3. Round the big numbers to friendly ones — usually the nearest thousand.把大數四捨五入成好算的數——通常到千位。
  4. Calculate with the friendly numbers.用好算的數計算。
  5. Pick the choice that is closest.選最接近的選項。

1Q2 — How many schools? Two steps, then round up第 2 題 — 需要幾所學校？兩個步驟，然後無條件進位

2

In a town, there are 1,349 families. If there are on average two children attending elementary school from each family and each school can accommodate 220 children, the minimum number of elementary schools needed in the region is .

a. 6b. 9c. 13
Two steps: children first (×2), then schools (÷220). Leftover children still need a school → round UP → c. 13
Word problems · page 1 · Q2
📘 Textbook version · 課本理論版
  • Read the last sentence first: it asks for the minimum number of schools. But the story gives families, not children — so this is a two-step question.先讀最後一句：題目要的是「最少需要幾所學校」。但題目給的是家庭數，不是孩子數——所以這題要分兩步。
  • Step 1 — children: 1,349 families × 2 = 2,698. Round it: about 2,700 children.第一步 — 孩子數：1,349 × 2 = 2,698，四捨五入：大約 2,700 個孩子。
  • Step 2 — schools: one school holds 220. 220 × 12 = 2,640 — that leaves 60 children with no school. 220 × 13 = 2,860 — now everyone fits. So 13 schools.第二步 — 學校數：一所 220 人。220 × 12 = 2,640，還剩 60 個孩子沒學校；220 × 13 = 2,860，大家都裝得下。所以 13 所。
  • Rule: when a question asks for the minimum number needed, always round up. Even 1 extra child needs a whole school. Answer: c. 13.規則：題目問「最少需要幾個」，一定往上進位。就算只多 1 個孩子，也需要一整所學校。答案：c. 13。
  • Check: 13 × 220 = 2,860 ≥ 2,698 ✓ and 12 × 220 = 2,640 is too small ✗.檢查：13 × 220 = 2,860 ≥ 2,698 ✓；12 × 220 = 2,640 太少 ✗。
🍎 Real-life version · 生活應用版
  • Picture 2,700 children going on a school trip. Each bus seats 220. You fill the buses one at a time.想像 2,700 個孩子要去校外教學，一台車坐 220 人，一台一台裝。
  • After 12 full buses (2,640 seats) there are still 60 children standing at the gate. You can't leave them behind — so you call a 13th bus, even if it's mostly empty.裝滿 12 台（2,640 個位子）後，還有 60 個孩子站在門口。不能把他們丟下——所以要叫第 13 台車，就算它很空。
  • Schools work the same way: leftover children = one more school.學校也一樣：多出來的孩子 ＝ 多一所學校。
  • Why double first? The story talks about families, but buses carry children. Always change to the thing you are counting before you divide.為什麼要先乘 2？題目講的是家庭，但車上坐的是孩子。除之前，一定先換成你要數的那樣東西。
  • Trick: "how many groups do I need?" → divide, and if anything is left over, add 1.口訣：「需要幾組？」→ 先除，有剩就再加 1。

2Q3 — Girls for every 1,000 families: find the rate, then multiply第 3 題 — 每 1,000 個家庭有幾個女嬰：先找每組幾個，再乘

3

In 2017, a survey finds that there are 34 babies born for every 1,000 families in a city. Among these babies, 20 of them are boys. There are 88,326 families in this city in 2017 and the total number of girls born in this year is about .

a. 1,200b. 1,500c. 1,800
Girls in every 1,000 = 34 − 20 = 14. 88,326 ≈ 88 thousands. 14 × 88 ≈ 1,200 → a
Word problems · page 1 · Q3
📘 Textbook version · 課本理論版
  • The question asks for girls, but it only tells you 34 babies and 20 boys. So first: girls for every 1,000 families = 34 − 20 = 14.題目問的是女嬰，但只告訴你 34 個嬰兒、20 個男嬰。所以先算：每 1,000 個家庭的女嬰 = 34 − 20 = 14。
  • "For every 1,000 families" means: count the groups of 1,000. 88,326 families ≈ 88,000 = 88 groups.「每 1,000 個家庭」的意思是：數數看有幾組 1,000。88,326 ≈ 88,000 ＝ 88 組。
  • Multiply: 14 girls × 88 groups = 1,232. Quick estimate: 14 × 90 = 1,260. Both are about 1,200. Answer: a. 1,200.相乘：14 × 88 = 1,232。快速估算：14 × 90 = 1,260。都大約是 1,200。答案：a. 1,200。
  • The trap: 20 boys × 88 = 1,760 ≈ 1,800 — that is choice c, but it counts the boys, not the girls.陷阱：20 × 88 = 1,760 ≈ 1,800——那是選項 c，但那算的是男嬰，不是女嬰。
  • Habit: underline what the question asks for (girls) before you touch any numbers.習慣：動手算之前，先把題目問的東西（girls）畫底線。
🍎 Real-life version · 生活應用版
  • Think of every 1,000 families as one sticker pack. Each pack holds 34 stickers: 20 blue ones (boys) and 14 pink ones (girls).把每 1,000 個家庭想成一包貼紙。每包 34 張：20 張藍的（男生）、14 張粉紅的（女生）。
  • 88,326 families is about 88 packs. The extra 326 families is less than half a pack, so you can ignore it.88,326 個家庭大約是 88 包。多出來的 326 不到半包，可以忽略。
  • Pink stickers altogether: 14 × 88. Easy way: 10 × 88 = 880 and 4 × 88 = 352, so 1,232. Or just 14 × 90 = 1,260. Either way, about 1,200.粉紅貼紙總共：14 × 88。簡單算法：10 × 88 = 880，4 × 88 = 352，加起來 1,232。或者直接 14 × 90 = 1,260。反正大約 1,200。
  • If you counted the blue stickers by mistake (20 × 88 = 1,760) you would land on 1,800 — the wrong colour!如果不小心數到藍色貼紙（20 × 88 = 1,760），就會選到 1,800——顏色錯了！
  • Trick: when you see "for every 1,000", ask "how many thousands do I have?" That number is your multiplier.口訣：看到「每 1,000 個」，就問自己「我有幾個 1,000？」那個數就是要乘的數。

3Q4 — The difference: round to the nearest thousand, then subtract第 4 題 — 兩城相差多少：先四捨五入到千位，再相減

4

The population of a city is 67,721 and the neighboring city has a population of 52,103. The difference between two cities is .

a. 13,000b. 14,00c. 16,000
Difference = subtract. Round to thousands: 68,000 − 52,000 = 16,000 → c
Word problems · page 2 · Q4
📘 Textbook version · 課本理論版
  • Difference means subtract: bigger − smaller → 67,721 − 52,103.Difference（差）就是相減：大的減小的 → 67,721 − 52,103。
  • Estimate: round each number to the nearest thousand — exactly what you did on your rounding page. 67,721 → 68,000 and 52,103 → 52,000.估算：把兩個數都四捨五入到千位——跟你那頁四捨五入一模一樣。67,721 → 68,000，52,103 → 52,000。
  • 68,000 − 52,000 = 16,000. Answer: c. 16,000.68,000 − 52,000 = 16,000。答案：c. 16,000。
  • Why not round to the nearest ten thousand? 70,000 − 50,000 = 20,000 — and 20,000 isn't even a choice. Too rough. The nearest thousand keeps enough detail.為什麼不四捨五入到萬位？70,000 − 50,000 = 20,000——選項裡根本沒有 20,000。太粗了。到千位才留得住足夠的細節。
  • Check with the exact answer: 67,721 − 52,103 = 15,618, and 15,618 is closest to 16,000 ✓.用精確答案檢查：67,721 − 52,103 = 15,618，最接近 16,000 ✓。
🍎 Real-life version · 生活應用版
  • Picture each city's people packed into bags of 1,000. City A: 67 full bags plus most of another one (721 people) → call it 68 bags. City B: 52 bags plus a little (103) → 52 bags.想像每個城市的人都裝進「一袋 1,000 人」的袋子。A 城：67 袋再加大半袋（721 人）→ 算 68 袋。B 城：52 袋多一點點（103 人）→ 算 52 袋。
  • How many more bags does A have? 68 − 52 = 16 bags → 16,000 people.A 比 B 多幾袋？68 − 52 = 16 袋 → 16,000 人。
  • If you used giant bags of 10,000 instead: 7 bags − 5 bags = 2 bags = 20,000. The bags are so big you can't see the answer any more. Bags of 1,000 are the right size here.如果改用 10,000 人的大袋子：7 袋 − 5 袋 = 2 袋 = 20,000。袋子太大，反而看不出答案。這題用 1,000 的袋子剛剛好。
  • Trick: the answer choices tell you the bag size. Choices like 13,000 / 16,000 → think in thousands.口訣：選項會告訴你袋子多大。選項是 13,000／16,000 這種 → 用「千」來想。

4Q5 — Increased by: which operation?第 5 題 — 人口增加了：該用加還是減？

5

In 2016, the population of a region was 91,274. In 2017, the population was increased by 6,015. In 2017, the population was about .

a. 85,000b. 97,300c. 99,800
"Increased by" = add. 91,000 + 6,000 = 97,000 → closest is b. 97,300
Word problems · page 2 · Q5
📘 Textbook version · 課本理論版
  • Increased by 6,015 means the population went up by 6,015. Went up = add.「increased by 6,015」表示人口往上多了 6,015。往上 ＝ 用加的。
  • Round to the nearest thousand: 91,274 → 91,000 and 6,015 → 6,000. Then 91,000 + 6,000 = 97,000.四捨五入到千位：91,274 → 91,000，6,015 → 6,000。然後 91,000 + 6,000 = 97,000。
  • Which choice is closest to 97,000? b. 97,300. (The exact answer is 97,289 — very close.)哪個選項最接近 97,000？b. 97,300。（精確答案是 97,289，非常接近。）
  • The trap: 85,000 is what you get if you subtract: 91,000 − 6,000. The word "increased" tells you not to.陷阱：85,000 是用減的（91,000 − 6,000）會得到的。「increased」這個字告訴你不能減。
  • Key words → operation: increased by / more / total / altogether → +. Decreased by / difference / fewer / how many more → −. Half → ÷ 2.關鍵字 → 運算：increased by、more、total、altogether → 加；decreased by、difference、fewer、how many more → 減；half → 除以 2。
🍎 Real-life version · 生活應用版
  • The region has about 91 bags of 1,000 people. In 2017 it got 6 more bags.這個地區有大約 91 袋 1,000 人。2017 年又多了 6 袋。
  • More bags → add: 91 + 6 = 97 bags = 97,000 people → the closest choice is 97,300.袋子變多 → 用加的：91 + 6 = 97 袋 = 97,000 人 → 最接近的選項是 97,300。
  • If 6 bags had been taken away, the story would say "decreased", and you'd have 85 bags. Not this time!如果是被拿走 6 袋，題目會說 decreased，那就剩 85 袋。這題不是！
  • Trick: read the story like a movie — is the number growing or shrinking? Growing = +, shrinking = −.口訣：把題目當電影看——數字是在變多還是變少？變多 ＝ 加，變少 ＝ 減。

5Q6 — Half of a big number: round first, then halve第 6 題 — 大數的一半：先四捨五入，再對半分

6

There are 26,358 children living in this town. Half of the children are boys. There are about boys living in this town.

a. 12,800b. 13,000c. 13,300
Round first: 26,358 → 26,000. Half of 26,000 = 13,000 → b
Word problems · page 2 · Q6
📘 Textbook version · 課本理論版
  • Half means divide by 2.Half（一半）就是除以 2。
  • Estimate: round first. 26,358 → nearest thousand → 26,000. Half of 26,000 = 13,000. Answer: b. 13,000.估算：先四捨五入。26,358 → 千位 → 26,000。26,000 的一半 ＝ 13,000。答案：b. 13,000。
  • To halve thousands fast, halve the number of thousands: half of 26 thousand = 13 thousand. Half of 48 thousand = 24 thousand. If it's odd, like 70 thousand, half is 35 thousand.快速對半分「幾千」：把幾千的數字對半分就好。26 千的一半 ＝ 13 千；48 千的一半 ＝ 24 千；奇數像 70 千，一半是 35 千。
  • Why round first? If you divide exactly you get 13,179, which sits between 13,000 and 13,300 — confusing. "About" questions want you to round first, and then the answer is clear: 13,000.為什麼要先四捨五入？精確算會得到 13,179，夾在 13,000 和 13,300 中間——很混亂。有「about」的題目就是要你先四捨五入，這樣答案就清楚了：13,000。
🍎 Real-life version · 生活應用版
  • 26,358 children ≈ 26 bags of 1,000. Boys and girls share the bags equally: 26 ÷ 2 = 13 bags each → 13,000 boys.26,358 個孩子 ≈ 26 袋 1,000。男生女生平分：26 ÷ 2 ＝ 一邊 13 袋 → 13,000 個男生。
  • The loose 358 children are less than half a bag — for an "about" answer, let them go.零散的 358 個孩子不到半袋——要的是「大約」，就不用管他們。
  • Halving bags is easy when the number of bags is even (26, 48, 90). If it's odd (70 bags), split one bag in half: 35 bags.袋數是偶數（26、48、90）時對半分很簡單。奇數（70 袋）的話，把一袋切一半：35 袋。
  • Trick: half = share between two. Round to bags first, then share the bags.口訣：一半 ＝ 兩個人平分。先四捨五入成幾袋，再平分袋子。
