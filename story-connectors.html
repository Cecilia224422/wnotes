Step 1

Two kinds of sentences. A list sentence lines actions up with "and" — each one stands alone. A combo sentence uses a connector to show how they link.句子有兩種。清單句用 and 把動作排成一排——每一招自己站著。連招句用連接詞，講出招式之間怎麼連。

Four hits with no link — the enemy resets after every one. A combo chains them: each move powers the next.四下攻擊沒有連起來——每打一下，敵人就重置一次。連招是把招式鏈起來：每一招都幫下一招充能。

👉 In your Quest 4 line, tap the word doing the lining-up.在你的 Quest 4 句子裡，點出那個「把招式排成一排」的字。

Step 2

Meet the two combo words for battle scenes. when = timing: two moves share the same moment. so = cause: one move makes the next one possible.認識戰鬥場景的兩個連招字。when＝時機：兩招共用同一個瞬間。so＝因果：前一招讓下一招變得可能。

when says "as this lands…". so says "…which opens the door for…". Two different links in the chain.when 是「就在這招落下的同時……」。so 是「……於是門就為下一招打開了」。鏈子上兩種不同的扣環。

"When Lloyd lifted an enemy into the air, Nya blasted two more" — what is when telling you?「When Lloyd lifted an enemy into the air, Nya blasted two more」——when 在告訴你什麼呢？

🔎 You first

Your battle already happened — you're telling it afterwards. Which tag fits: says or said?你的戰鬥已經發生過了——你是事後在講它。哪個標籤合身呢：says 還是 said？

Step 1

After the quotation marks comes a little label that tells who spoke. That label is the speech tag.引號後面接著一個小標籤，說出是誰講的話。那個標籤就叫 speech tag。

Every line of battle dialogue wears a name tag, so the reader knows whose mouth it came out of.每一句戰鬥台詞都掛著名牌，讀者才知道是從誰的嘴巴出來的。

👉 In your opening line, tap the speech tag.在你的開場句裡，點出 speech tag。

Your Quest 4 opening“Let's go! Ninjas and Baymax!” Cole shouted.

Step 2

A verb wears a tense — its time. shouted wears the past: it already happened. says wears the present: happening right now.動詞會穿著「時態」——它的時間。shouted 穿的是過去式：已經發生了。says 穿的是現在式：正在發生。

Every tag carries a time stamp: BACK THEN or RIGHT NOW. Stamp these two.每個標籤都帶著時間印章：「當時」或「現在」。幫下面兩個蓋章吧。

Step 3 · say it your way
