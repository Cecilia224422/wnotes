📘The habit要養成的習慣

Three rules you built yourself — one per trap. Open only the one you need.

三條你親手推出來的規則，一種陷阱一條。需要哪條再點開哪條。

The direction rule方向規則
Fix the word’s + or − before you read the options.先確定單字的＋或−，再去看選項。

Check: did you tag the word first?檢查：你先給單字貼標籤了嗎？

The familiar-word rule熟字不一定是答案
A familiar choice is not automatically a meaning match.看得懂的選項，不一定和題目字同義。

Learn the question word first. Then compare every choice.先把題目字的意思弄懂，再逐一比較選項。

The picture rule畫面規則
Picture the motion before you match any word.先在腦中畫出移動方向，再配單字。

Check: does your word move the same way as the sentence?檢查：你選的字和句子移動的方向相同嗎？
