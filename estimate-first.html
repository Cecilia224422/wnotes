Step 1 · how many hundreds hide in 2,400?

2,400 is 2 thousands and 4 hundreds. Each thousand holds 10 hundreds — so that's 20 hundreds, plus 4 more. Your line 1 already knew this.2,400 是 2 個千和 4 個百。每個千裡面藏著 10 個百——所以是 20 個百，再加 4 個。你的第一行早就知道這件事囉。

👉 So 2,400 = ___ hundreds.所以 2,400 = ___ 個 hundred。

Step 2 · divide the hundreds, keep the name

Share 24 hundreds among 6: the 24 gets divided, but the name hundreds stays stuck on. 24 hundreds ÷ 6 = 4 hundreds — never a bare 4.把 24 個百分給 6：被除的是 24，但「hundreds」這個名字是黏在上面的。24 hundreds ÷ 6 = 4 個「百」——絕對不是光溜溜的 4。

Step 1 · why round-after can never argue

Look at what got rounded: the answer you had just written. Rounding a number only makes a tidier copy of it — and a copy always agrees with its original. Right answer → tidy. Wrong answer → still tidy.看看被四捨五入的是什麼：是「你剛剛才寫下來的答案」。把一個數四捨五入，只會做出它比較整齊的複本——而複本永遠同意本尊。答案對→很整齊。答案錯→還是很整齊。

Shout 200 into a canyon — the echo says 200. Shout a wrong number, and the echo repeats that too. An echo can't argue with you.對著山谷大喊 200——回音就喊 200。喊一個錯的數字，回音也照樣重複。回音沒辦法跟你唱反調呀。
