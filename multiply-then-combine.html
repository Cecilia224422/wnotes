📘The habit這次的習慣

Two groups make two subtotals. The last words decide what happens between them.

兩組資料就要有兩個小計；最後一句決定兩個小計要怎麼合併。

Total → add the subtotals總計 → 小計相加
When the problem asks for total points or total cost, add every required subtotal once.
題目問總分或總費用時，每個需要的小計都要加一次。
How much more → subtract the totals多多少 → 兩個總額相減
Different quantities mean you must find both sales totals first. Then subtract smaller from larger.
數量不同時，要先算兩邊的銷售總額，再用大減小。
