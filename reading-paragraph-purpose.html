📘Two questions before choosing選答案前先問兩句

What does the evidence show? Translate the exact action or phrase without adding a stronger feeling.

證據顯示什麼？只翻譯原本的動作或語句，不自己加上更強烈的感受。

What job does this paragraph do? Name the main change: introduce danger, describe a reaction, or contrast it with another reaction.

這段做什麼工作？說出主要變化：引入危險、描寫反應，或和另一種反應形成對比。
