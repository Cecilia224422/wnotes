Which one is an artifact — something a person made or used?哪一樣是 artifact——「人」做的或用過的？

Step 1

Your book says an artifact is an object made or used by people — tools, pottery, weapons, baskets.課本說：artifact 是「人」做的或用過的東西——工具、陶器、武器、籃子。

Detective rule #1: an artifact has human fingerprints on it — somebody made it, or somebody used it.偵探守則一：artifact 上面有「人的痕跡」——有人做了它，或有人用過它。

👉 Tap the 3 words that make something an artifact.點出讓東西成為 artifact 的三個關鍵詞。

A fossil is nature's leftover — like a footprint left in mud that turned to stone. So who made it?化石是大自然留下的東西——像泥巴上的腳印後來變成石頭。那它是誰做的？

🔎 You first

Here is your fixed sentence:這是你改完的句子：

Learning to farm had little change on the nomadic way of life.

Is it TRUE now?它現在是「真的」了嗎？

Step 1

The task says: rewrite to make it true. Not "make it different" — make it TRUE. That almost always means the opposite meaning.題目要求：改寫讓句子「變成真的」。不是「換個說法」——是變成真的。這幾乎都表示要改成相反的意思。

A sign says "Pool CLOSED" but the pool is open. Writing "Pool SHUT" fixes nothing — you must write "OPEN".告示牌寫「泳池關閉」，但泳池其實開放。改成「泳池不開」還是錯——必須寫「開放」。

So your rewrite must…所以你的改寫必須……

Step 2

What does the lesson say? Farming gave people a steady food supply, so they could settle in one place. So farming's impact on nomadic life was…課文說：農耕帶來穩定的食物，人們可以定居下來。所以農耕對游牧生活的影響是……

Before farming: pack up all year, chase the herds. After farming: build a home and stay. That kind of change is…農耕前：整年打包、追著獸群跑。農耕後：蓋房子、住下來。這種改變是……

Flip "little impact" → "a big impact". Then run the re-read test: read the WHOLE new sentence.把 little impact 翻成 a big impact。然後做「重讀測試」：整句重新讀一次。
