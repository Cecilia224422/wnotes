📘Two questions before choosing選答案前先問兩句

What does the evidence show? Translate the exact action or phrase without adding a stronger feeling.

證據顯示什麼？只翻譯原本的動作或語句，不自己加上更強烈的感受。

What job does this paragraph do? Name the main change: introduce danger, describe a reaction, contrast reactions, or resolve a problem and show the result.

這段做什麼工作？說出主要變化：引入危險、描寫反應、對比反應，或解決問題並顯示結果。
