📘 Textbook version· 課本理論版
  • Equivalent fractions show the same amount with different numbers. You make one by multiplying the top and the bottom by the same number. 等值分數：一樣多，只是數字不同。做法是分子和分母乘以同一個數。
  • To compare two fractions, first make the denominators the same. Then compare the numerators. 要比較兩個分數，先把分母變一樣，再比分子。
  • Find a common denominator: a common multiple of 4 and 3. 4, 8, 12… and 3, 6, 9, 12… → 12! (Common multiples — from last week.) 找公分母：4 和 3 的公倍數。4、8、12…和 3、6、9、12… → 12！（上週學的公倍數。）
  • 3/4 = 9/12 (×3 on top and bottom).   2/3 = 8/12 (×4 on top and bottom). 3/4 = 9/12（上下都 ×3）。2/3 = 8/12（上下都 ×4）。
  • Same bottom now, so compare the tops: 9 > 8. So 3/4 is greater than 2/3. 分母一樣了，比分子：9 > 8。所以 3/4 比 2/3 大。
  • Check habit: before you compare, ask "Are the bottoms the same?" If not — not yet! 檢查習慣：比較之前先問「分母一樣了嗎？」還沒一樣就還不能比。
🍎 Real-life version· 生活應用版
  • Two chocolate bars, same size. Bar A is cut into 4 rows and you get 3 rows (3/4). Bar B is cut into 3 rows and you get 2 rows (2/3). Who gets more? 兩條一樣大的巧克力。A 切成 4 排，你拿 3 排（3/4）。B 切成 3 排，你拿 2 排（2/3）。誰拿得多？
  • Counting pieces alone doesn't work: 4 sixths vs 6 eighths is like 4 big cookies vs 6 small cookies. The pieces are different sizes. 只數塊數沒用：4 個六分之一 vs 6 個八分之一，就像 4 塊大餅乾 vs 6 塊小餅乾，每塊大小不同。
  • So cut both bars into the same little squares — 12 squares each. Bar A: 3 rows × 3 = 9 squares. Bar B: 2 rows × 4 = 8 squares. 9 > 8! 所以把兩條都切成一樣的小方塊——各 12 塊。A：3 排 × 3 = 9 塊。B：2 排 × 4 = 8 塊。9 > 8！
  • Another way to see it: 3/4 is missing one fourth; 2/3 is missing one third. A third is a bigger bite missing, so 2/3 is the smaller one. 另一種看法：3/4 少了一個四分之一；2/3 少了一個三分之一。三分之一少得比較多，所以 2/3 比較小。
  • Trick: same bottom first. Can't think of a common multiple? Multiply the two bottoms: 4 × 3 = 12 always works. 口訣：先同分母。想不到公倍數？把兩個分母相乘：4 × 3 = 12 一定可以。
