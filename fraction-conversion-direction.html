📘The two-way map雙向轉換地圖

Improper → mixed: divide numerator by denominator; quotient is the whole, remainder is the numerator.

假分數 → 帶分數：分子÷分母；商是整數、餘數是新分子。

Mixed → improper: whole × denominator + numerator; keep the denominator.

帶分數 → 假分數：整數×分母＋分子；分母保持不變。

Then simplify without changing the requested form.

最後化簡，但不要把題目要的形式改掉。
