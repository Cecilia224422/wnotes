🔎 You first

That line at the top of p.49 — what happened?p.49 最上面那一行——當時發生了什麼事？

All three of these are real answers. Pick yours and I will take you the right way round.這三個都是真的答案。挑你的那一個，我就走那條路帶你。

Step 1 · what one box means

In a bar model every box is the same size. One box is 1 unit. The smallest amount in the problem is 1 unit, and everything else is built from copies of it.bar model 裡每一格都一樣大。一格就是 1 unit。題目裡最小的那個量當作 1 unit，其他東西都用它複製出來。

Think LEGO. One brick is 1 unit. "Three times as many" means three of the same brick — you never make one fat brick instead.想像樂高。一塊積木＝1 unit。「3 倍」的意思是「同樣的積木三塊」——不是把一塊做胖。

👉 Tap the 3 chips that describe 1 unit.點出描述 1 unit 的三個詞。

Step 2 · who is 1 unit?

The problem says "Thomas baked 3 times as many as Riley". Riley is the one being compared to — so Riley is the small bar.題目說「Thomas 是 Riley 的 3 倍」。被拿來比較的那一個是 Riley——所以 Riley 是小的那條。

Riley's tray is the tray you photocopy. Thomas's pile is three of Riley's trays, not one mysterious big tray.Riley 的托盤就是你要拿去影印的那一盤。Thomas 那堆是「三盤 Riley」，不是一個神秘的大盤子。

So in question 1, who is 1 unit?所以第 1 題裡，誰是 1 unit？

Step 3 · how many copies?

"3 times as many" means 3 units. Copy Riley's box, keep it exactly the same size, and stop when the picture matches the words.「3 倍」＝3 units。把 Riley 那格複製，大小保持一模一樣，圖跟文字對上就停。

You are the photocopier. Press the button, out comes the same tray. Press it until the pile matches the sentence — then stop.你就是影印機。按一下，出來一模一樣的一盤。按到跟句子一樣多就停。

👉 Copy Riley's box for Thomas, then press Done when the picture matches the problem.幫 Thomas 複製 Riley 的格子，圖跟題目對上時按 Done。

Look at the boxes and just read them out: three trays, 185 in each. What do you do with three lots of 185?看著格子直接念出來：三盤，每盤 185。三份 185 要用什麼算？

In long division, how do you know you have finished?長除法要怎麼知道自己「做完了」？

Step 1 · what you already wrote

Long division repeats four moves: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down. You ran that loop twice and both rounds are correct.長除法重複四個動作：除、乘、減、把下一位拉下來。這個循環你跑了兩輪，兩輪都對。

It is like unpacking a box in rounds: take out what fits, see what is left, pull the next thing down. You did two rounds perfectly.像分批清空一個箱子：拿走裝得下的，看剩什麼，再把下一個拉下來。你完美跑完兩輪。

👉 In the working at the bottom of this box, tap the number that is still waiting to be divided.在這個方框最下面的算式裡，點出「還在等著被除」的那個數字。

Extra Practice 4A · p.49–53 · Q8
🔎 You first

Two answers, both from your own pages. Which one tells the whole story?兩個答案，都出自你自己的紙上。哪一個把話講完了？

Step 1 · half an answer

A word problem asks about real things, so the answer is a number and its label: 555 muffins, 800 cm, $2,020. The number alone is half the answer.應用題問的是真實的東西，所以答案是「數字＋單位」：555 muffins、800 cm、$2,020。只寫數字等於只寫了一半。

If I texted you just 15, you would text back "15 what?" Minutes? Dollars? Muffins? A bare number is a riddle.如果我只傳「15」給你，你一定會回「15 什麼？」分鐘？錢？muffin？光一個數字就是謎語。

👉 The question asks how much Ms. Lee paid. Tap the finished answer.題目問 Ms. Lee 付了多少。點出「寫完了」的那個答案。

Step 2 · where labels come from

You never invent a label. It is already printed in the question — muffins, centimetres, stamps, dollars. Copy it down with the number.單位不用發明。它早就印在題目裡了——muffins、公分、郵票、錢。連著數字一起抄下來就對了。

The label is not yours to choose, like a name tag at a party: you do not make one up, you pick up the one with your name already on it.單位不是你選的，像派對上的名牌：你不會自己編一個，你會去拿寫著你名字的那一個。

So where do you look for the right label?那要去哪裡找對的單位？

Step 3 · the out-loud check

Before you close the book, read your answer aloud. If it sounds unfinished — "2,020… what?" — it is unfinished.闔上本子前，把答案念出來。如果聽起來沒講完——「2,020……什麼？」——那就是還沒寫完。

Say it to an imaginary friend who did not read the question. If they would say "huh?", the label is missing.念給一個沒讀過題目的想像朋友聽。如果他會說「蛤？」，就是少了單位。

Step 4 · say it your way

Your 2,020 was the right number. So why can a bare number still lose a mark?用你自己的話選：你的 2,020 數字是對的，那為什麼還是可能被扣分？

🧰 Four habits to carry into the next set
  1. Bar model first, number sentence second. Read, draw, label the bars — then choose × or ÷.先畫 bar model，再寫算式：讀題、畫格、標數字，最後才決定用乘還是除。
  2. Find 1 unit. In any "times as many" or "half as many" problem, the smallest bar is 1 unit. Draw that one first.找出 1 unit：只要出現「幾倍」「一半」，最小的那條就是 1 unit，先畫它。
  3. Never stop a long division early. Keep subtracting until the bottom line is written — even when you can already see the answer.長除法不准提早停：一直減到最後一行寫出來，就算你早就看出答案了。
  4. Ask "what is the unit?" before you close the book: dollars, cm, boxes, photos, stamps, muffins.闔上本子前問一句「單位是什麼？」——錢、公分、盒、照片、郵票、muffin。
🧲 Why the bar model is worth it, even when it feels too easyIt is not there to slow you down. It is the tool that rescues you later, on the question where one total is shared three ways and one person gets double and another gets half. If you skip it while the questions are easy, it will not be in your hands when a question really needs it. Practise it while it feels too easy.bar model 不是來拖慢你的。它是以後救你的工具——那種「一個總數分給三個人、一個拿兩倍、一個拿一半」的題目。簡單的時候不畫，難的時候它就不在你手上。趁著覺得太簡單的時候練起來。
