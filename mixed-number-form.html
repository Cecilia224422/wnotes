📘The habit這次的習慣

The arithmetic was usually available. The answer form named in the directions was the repeated trap.

計算通常做得到；重複出現的陷阱是沒有照題目指定的答案形式作答。

Pictures: count parts, not circles看圖：數小份，不是數圓圈
Three partly shaded circles are not automatically three wholes. Count all shaded fourths or fifths first.
三個部分塗色的圓不會自動變成三個整數；要先數全部塗色的四分之一或五分之一。
Number line: count intervals數線：數區間
Start from the whole number and count the spaces to the arrow. Do not count the starting tick as one step.
從整數開始數到箭頭的空格；起點刻度不算第一步。
Obey the requested form照題目要求的形式作答
Mixed number: keep the whole. Simplest form: reduce the fraction part and keep the whole.
要求帶分數：整數留下。要求最簡形式：把分數部分約分，整數仍留下。
