💡Four last checks四個最後檢查

Question sentence? Circle it and give the unknown a letter.

“Less than” or “fewer than”? Start from the bigger amount, then subtract.

“Times as many”? Draw the bars and count all the units before writing the equation.

Two steps? Find the hidden number first, then write the whole model, then check.

圈出問句並給未知數一個字母；less than／fewer than 從較大的量開始減；times as many 先畫線段圖、數完全部單位再列式；兩步驟題先找隱藏數，再寫完整模型並驗算。
